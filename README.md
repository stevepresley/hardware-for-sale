## Information needed for the Synology listing

Please provide:

- A photo or transcription of **DSM Control Panel → Info Center** showing the model and chassis serial number.
- Confirmed installed RAM amount and module configuration.
- What is included in the sale: chassis, power supply, trays, drives, and which drives (if any) will be removed.
- Physical condition and any known faults beyond the identified failing 18 TB drive.
- Whether the paid Surveillance Station 1-camera and 4-camera packs will be included; do not post license keys publicly.
